CoffeeChat AI

A personal networking workflow for preparing thoughtful questions, organizing calls, and following through after a conversation.

Problem
Preparing for informational interviews in a second language took time. I wanted support with clear questions and a consistent way to follow up after networking calls.
Approach
Built a workflow that helps prepare for meetings, generate relevant questions, remember follow-ups, and draft emails based on questions and call notes. Used Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, and Claude Code to turn my own networking needs into a working product.
Result
I use it to streamline meeting preparation and follow-up. Building it strengthened my coding, AI prompting, and product-management skills—from defining a personal problem to designing and refining a complete workflow.

Scripture Primer

A bilingual learning product that helps readers understand scripture stories before approaching the full text.

Problem
Long, unfamiliar texts can create a high barrier for new readers who lack the story context needed to orient themselves.
Approach
Used Claude Code, Supabase, and Vercel to build short story primers, chronological navigation, quizzes, flashcards, character references, reading tools, and search in Chinese and English.
Result
Went from starting with little familiarity with scripture stories to preparing to teach and share them during missionary service. I now share the live product with friends and religion professors for learning and feedback.

My first corporate job, in four steps

I came in new to cost models and corporate work. Here’s how I learned on the job.

  1. Ask

    Learn from the team

    I learned to ask good questions, speak up with confidence, and build relationships across the team.

  2. Go see

    Visit suppliers

    I went to suppliers to understand what actually drives their cost, and learned the math behind cost models.

  3. Build$550K+

    Should-cost analysis across cutting, machining, welding and painting flagged supplier-rate anomalies and identified this much in cost-reduction opportunities.

  4. Repeat

    Make it easier next time

    I built AI agents for future should-cost work and automated safety-stock checks, giving the team a new way to use AI.
